Velour

Client
Velour Lingerie (concept)
Year
2026
Scope
Identity, Packaging, Print, Digital

A brand identity for a Ukrainian lingerie label. The idea is softness and comfort, so I chose powder pink and warm nude tones, a V monogram with a leaf branch and a light serif wordmark. I built the whole system: logo, colours and fonts, line and floral elements, a pattern, stationery, gift boxes and shopping bags, merch, signage and templates for Instagram, email and the website.

Process

From idea to brand

Velour was my bachelor thesis: a complete identity for a lingerie brand, from market research to the logo, the visual system and every place the brand appears.

01

Research

I started with the market. What makes lingerie brands recognisable, and where is there room for something new?

Analysed
Victoria’s Secret, Calvin Klein, Intimissimi, Women’secret: logos, colours, typography, photo style
Insight
Most brands are either loud and glamorous or strict and minimal. Velour sits in between: soft, calm and feminine
Concept
Premium minimalism. Comfort, care and confidence, with lots of white space and pastel tones
02

Logo

A stylised V inside a thin open circle, with a leaf branch for softness and nature. The wordmark is a classic serif. I developed a main version and additional versions for small sizes and different layouts.

03

Colours, type & pattern

Powder pink and warm nude tones, a serif for headlines and a clean sans for text. The line elements, frames and the pattern all come from the leaves in the logo, so everything feels like one family.

04

Print & packaging

Business card, letterhead, envelope, tags and gift card in one consistent style. The gift boxes are made from dense, textured board with the pattern and an embossed logo; the shopping bags carry the same pattern.

05

Brand in use

Merch for staff and events, the shop sign and a full set of digital templates: Instagram, stories, email banners and the online shop.
