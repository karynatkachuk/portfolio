Velour

Client
Velour Lingerie (concept)
Year
2026
Scope
Identity, Packaging, Print, Digital

A brand identity for a Ukrainian lingerie label. The idea is softness and comfort, so I chose powder pink and warm nude tones, a V monogram with a leaf branch and a light serif wordmark. I built the whole system: logo, colours and fonts, line and floral elements, a pattern, stationery, gift boxes and shopping bags, merch, signage and templates for Instagram, email and the website.
